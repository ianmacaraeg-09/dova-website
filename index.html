002 What is DOVA

DOVA is a founder-led studio that builds to fit, not to template.

The problem

Off-the-shelf software makes you change how you work to suit the tool. Painful at ten staff, where the workaround everyone knows about is what keeps the business running.

Our approach

We build the other way round: your process first, the system shaped to it. Presets — a website and proven automations — are the starting point, not the product. Local realities come standard: BIR receipting, GCash and card, offline-first when the connection drops.

Your AI agent

Same engine. Two outputs. The same AI-augmented process DOVA uses to build a client's website and systems is also what becomes that client's backend agents. It's what lets a small studio ship a working build in weeks — not because it's cheaper, but because it's faster and does more.
